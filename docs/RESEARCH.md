The state-separated selection/test design, 20% alert budget, 30% recall floor, conservative county scope and calendar-week bootstrap are **explicit experiment/engineering hypotheses**, not externally validated operational standards. No empirical injury labels exist to justify changing the index weights, so those transparent fixed weights were retained. Missing components never receive redistributed weight. Matching county scope replaces the unsupported 150 km transfer assumption and ensures the served representative features actually match evaluation.

## Results that constrain the product

The original benchmark was numerically reproduced, archived and checksum-verified. The expanded sample doubled counties and extended the period without future realized-weather predictors. The final test was untouched by fitting/selection. Increased calibration events (58 flood/108 storm versus four each originally) improve the opportunity to diagnose calibration, not proof of calibration quality.

Flood retained the frozen estimator; changing its threshold reduced false alerts and recall without improving ranking. Storm selected LightGBM on validation but had worse same-population final AP and Brier than the original, with intervals spanning no change. Its independent geographic test missed all three reported events. The dashboard therefore presents mixed results, sparse geographic events and restrictive inference rather than a national-coverage claim. No follow-up model tuning used these final outcomes.

## Reproducibility and source integrity

`scripts/common.py` stores raw bytes, full requested URLs/parameters, UTC retrieval time, byte size and SHA-256 sidecars; cached checksums are verified before use. Corruption fails with an explicit file/manifest removal instruction; never delete runtime reports to repair research data. The selected NOAA filenames are 2024 c20260728, 2025 c20260819 and 2026 c20260918. The Census reference is 2024. Expanded collection made 24 serialized long-range requests with ten-second spacing, a bounded noncommercial request budget, and rate-limit handling; no paid service was called.

`data/training-provenance.json` records all 28 source manifests and transformations; `data/regions.json` freezes population and representative points. Exact bytes are retained in the local ignored raw cache. Different NOAA revisions can change outcomes; retain those bytes for exact reconstruction. No synthetic events, positives, target-weather observations or image-derived labels enter model training. The Jev test corpus is separately labeled hypothetical in tests/fixtures and never mixed with NOAA data.
