## Acquisition options and blockers

| Prospective USGS observations | Discover stream sites through OGC v1 `monitoring-locations` and `time-series-metadata`. Use `latest-continuous` for current status and paginated `continuous` intervals for the required history. | Operational subdaily meteorology | Acquire HRRR CONUS surface GRIB2 files and their inventories from `s3://noaa-hrrr-bdp-pds/hrrr.YYYYMMDD/conus/`. NOAA links cloud archives since 2014. [NOAA HRRR access](https://rapidrefresh.noaa.gov/hrrr/), [product inventory](https://www.nco.ncep.noaa.gov/pmb/products/hrrr/). | Run-specific rainfall and temperature for a separately frozen weather experiment. | Verify each accumulation interval, cycle and lead. Basin-average the contributing watershed rather than sampling the facility point. Weather features would change the candidate and require development before a fresh confirmation freeze. |

NWM's short-range forcing files are another subdaily option. The [published file contract](https://github.com/awslabs/open-data-docs/tree/main/docs/noaa/noaa-nwm-pds) includes `RAINRATE` and `T2D`, alongside channel streamflow. First verify that the chosen Google archive period actually retains those forcing files. Do not infer forcing coverage from a channel-file listing. HRRR precipitation also needs interval-aware extraction: NOAA's [forecast-hour-two inventory](https://www.nco.ncep.noaa.gov/pmb/products/hrrr/hrrr.t00z.wrfsfcf02.grib2.shtml) includes accumulated precipitation over hours zero through two. Summing cumulative fields would double-count rainfall.

NWM is an operational comparator, not independent observed truth. NOAA documents USGS streamflow assimilation in its analyses and forecast initialization. Preserve configuration and assimilation provenance, especially where the evaluation gauge supplies observations. Score future NWM discharge against the same approved USGS onset definition, with frozen units, time alignment and alert-policy selection. Do not substitute an analysis or retrospective simulation for a forecast. [NOAA configuration descriptions](https://water.noaa.gov/about/nwm), [retrospective dataset definition](https://registry.opendata.aws/nwm-archive/).

## Independent temporal and watershed opportunities

The first feasible temporal study is a prospectively frozen period on existing gauges. It can establish performance on new time periods and measure actual input eligibility, but cannot establish new-geography transfer. Choose the calendar interval and stopping rule before labels arrive. Keep later approved outcomes separate from issue-time input snapshots. A recent year is not automatically untouched; check the complete experiment exposure ledger before reserving any historical period.

For new geography, expand beyond CAMELS using the current USGS monitoring-location and series inventories. The [USGS location schema](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/ogcapi/v1/collections/monitoring-locations/schema?f=html) supplies HUC, coordinates, drainage-area and altitude metadata. [GAGES-II](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/gages-ii-geospatial-attributes-gages-evaluating-streamflow) adds downloadable basin boundaries, mainstem lines and reference-condition classifications. Its classification reflects an older inventory, so it is a candidate-discovery aid, not evidence of current telemetry, regulation or hourly completeness. Match current station identity and model attribute definitions before using it.

The research owner should acquire metadata and basin geometry first, then publish an eligibility ledger against every previous exposure. Preserve the [existing polygon audit requirements](HIGH_FLOW_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md#evidence-on-geographic-independence). Reserve candidates in unexposed HUC4s with disjoint contributing areas where feasible. No eligible broader-network cohort or candidate count has been established here. Historical reference discharge may be acquired under the existing protocol before confirmation outcomes, but recent outcome availability must not become a performance-based selection rule.

A same-HUC4 polygon study is a separate, narrower opportunity. It could establish transfer between drainage-separated basins inside an exposed region if the full prior-exposure intersection audit passes. It cannot satisfy an unseen-HUC4 claim. Any protocol authorizing that different claim must precede outcome inspection and retain the existing promotion requirements. Do not silently relabel this as the original geographic confirmation.

## Site applicability and the approved-only claim boundary

A research gauge and a facility need separate applicability review. Resolve the actual work area and receiving water to a reach, then inspect upstream and downstream connectivity. USGS NLDI supports mainstem, tributary and diversion navigation, but warns that catchment-indexed results can include off-flowpath features. Verify the precise position on the reach rather than trusting a returned station list. [Navigation documentation](https://api.water.usgs.gov/docs/nldi/navigation/).

The proposed applicability record must state whether the gauge is upstream, downstream or disconnected; its network distance; intervening tributaries, dams and diversions; and the drainage area represented relative to the site. An upstream gauge omits intervening inflow. A downstream gauge includes additional drainage and supplies no automatic upstream warning lead. Neither relationship justifies transplanting the gauge's percentile threshold to the work area. Use full-resolution [point-specific basin delineation](https://api.water.usgs.gov/docs/nldi/basin/) and obtain hydrologic review of routing, travel time, regulation, backwater and tidal effects. Network topology alone cannot certify those effects or a safe distance cutoff.

The current [observation parser and feature builder](../scripts/shift_hydrology.py) reject provisional or qualified discharge and require complete lag history. Its [protocol](SHIFT_HYDROLOGY_PROTOCOL.md) owns the fixed daily-reference period, quiet interval and receipt-delay assumption. The locked LightGBM can support only its documented retrospective development comparisons. Prospective capture can measure received data and withholding immediately. It can evaluate unchanged-model predictions only where every required approved input was actually received before issuance. Later approval cannot retroactively make a provisional input eligible.

Changing to provisional inputs needs an explicit new quality contract and evaluation of revision effects, calibration and coverage. It is not a compatible substitution for this candidate. Until that work passes confirmation, retain unavailable inputs as unavailable and withhold unsupported model output. Even a statistical pass would establish gauge high-flow onset performance, not verified site flooding, inundation depth, crew safety or a replacement for deterministic JHA rules.

The next acquisition decision is therefore to reserve geography from metadata, start issue-time USGS and NWM capture, and audit the Google archive's cycle coverage before committing to a historical operational comparison. Freeze artifacts, comparators, thresholds, exclusions and all existing gates before opening final labels. Report insufficient approved-input coverage or too few events as inconclusive; acquisition success alone grants no model promotion.
